# Technical SEO and content evidence — 17 October 2026

Production intent is discoverable fictional-product education with an honest bridge to the real ISMAIL portfolio. PROWORKS does not target purchase intent through fake pricing, reviews, subscriptions or business claims. Indexability is not proof of Google indexing or rankings.

## Implemented contract

Twelve distinct core purposes and three useful original articles have unique titles/descriptions, one H1, prerendered content, canonical/OG URLs at the target HTTPS origin, local social assets and accurate WebPage/Article JSON-LD. Article bylines and review dates describe the actual ISMAIL project editorial work. Breadcrumbs appear on articles; no fictional SoftwareApplication ratings/offers are generated.

Default builds, non-main Worker builds and 404 content are noindex. Preview builds omit sitemap links, sitemap files and robots sitemap declarations. Explicit production-mode builds generate exactly 15 canonical content URLs. The Worker preserves 404 status and noindex headers; live hosted verification is a separate release gate.

The five changed slugs have 301 aliases in `public/_redirects`. Cross-host redirects require the old Worker handler; an asset `_redirects` file does not establish old-domain routing. Do not activate it until the new host passes release checks.
